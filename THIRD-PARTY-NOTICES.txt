PictureSharp.Jxl 0.2.0

No native libraries are bundled. PictureSharp is a NuGet dependency distributed
separately under MIT. Project scaffolding and test patterns are adapted from
PictureSharp.Heif and PictureSharp.Tiff, Copyright (c) 2026 Artur Pelzak, MIT (see LICENSE).

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
libjxl v0.12.0 (commit a7a9c787341cf703dede03c2009fa460cae5e5df)
https://github.com/libjxl/libjxl

The following parts are scalar C# ports of libjxl sources and keep their origin
in source comments:
- src/PictureSharp.Jxl/Codestream/JxlBitReader.cs: lib/jxl/dec_bit_reader.h
- src/PictureSharp.Jxl/Codestream/JxlFields.cs: lib/jxl/fields.cc, fields.h,
  frame_header.h (U32, U64, F16, Enum, extensions, name strings)
- src/PictureSharp.Jxl/Codestream/JxlSizeHeader.cs: lib/jxl/headers.cc
- src/PictureSharp.Jxl/Codestream/JxlImageMetadata.cs and
  JxlCustomTransformData.cs: lib/jxl/image_metadata.cc
- src/PictureSharp.Jxl/Codestream/JxlColorEncoding.cs:
  lib/jxl/color_encoding_internal.cc, lib/jxl/cms/color_encoding_cms.h
- src/PictureSharp.Jxl/Codestream/JxlHeaders.cs and Container/JxlContainer.cs:
  header order, box rules and jxlp ordering of lib/jxl/decode.cc
