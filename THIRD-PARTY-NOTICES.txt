PictureSharp.Jxl 0.1.0

The scaffold contains no ported JPEG XL codec implementation and no bundled native libraries.
JPEG XL signature constants follow the format description:
https://github.com/libjxl/libjxl/blob/v0.12.0/doc/format_overview.md

PictureSharp is a NuGet dependency distributed separately under MIT.
Project scaffolding and test patterns are adapted from PictureSharp.Heif and
PictureSharp.Tiff, Copyright (c) 2026 Artur Pelzak, MIT (see LICENSE).
