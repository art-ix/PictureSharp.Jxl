PictureSharp.Jxl 0.6.1

No native libraries are bundled. PictureSharp is a NuGet dependency distributed
separately under MIT. Project scaffolding and test patterns are adapted from
PictureSharp.Heif and PictureSharp.Tiff, Copyright (c) 2026 Artur Pelzak, MIT (see LICENSE).

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
libjxl v0.12.0 (commit a7a9c787341cf703dede03c2009fa460cae5e5df)
https://github.com/libjxl/libjxl

The following parts under src/PictureSharp.Jxl are scalar C# ports of libjxl
sources. The modular refactoring preserves their algorithms and source attribution:
- Codestream/IO/JxlBitReader.cs: lib/jxl/dec_bit_reader.h
- Codestream/IO/JxlFields.cs: lib/jxl/fields.cc, fields.h, frame_header.h
- Codestream/Model size/preview/animation descriptors and their IO readers:
  lib/jxl/headers.cc
- Codestream/Model image metadata/custom transform descriptors and their IO
  readers: lib/jxl/image_metadata.cc, cms/opsin_params.h, quantizer.h
- Codestream/Model colour descriptors and their IO readers:
  lib/jxl/color_encoding_internal.cc, cms/color_encoding_cms.h
- Codestream/IO/JxlHeadersReader.cs and Container/JxlContainer.cs:
  lib/jxl/decode.cc (header order, box rules, jxlp ordering)
- Codestream/Entropy/JxlPrefixCode.cs: lib/jxl/dec_huffman.cc, huffman_table.cc
- Codestream/Entropy/JxlEntropyCode.cs and JxlSymbolReader.cs:
  lib/jxl/dec_ans.cc, dec_ans.h, ans_common.cc, ans_common.h, dec_context_map.cc,
  inverse_mtf-inl.h
- Codestream/Model frame/filter/pass/blending descriptors and their IO readers:
  lib/jxl/frame_header.cc, frame_header.h, frame_dimensions.h, loop_filter.cc,
  loop_filter.h
- Codestream/Frames/JxlTocReader.cs and Codestream/Entropy/JxlPermutation.cs:
  lib/jxl/toc.cc, coeff_order.cc, lehmer_code.h
- Codestream/Modular/JxlMaTree.cs, JxlModularStream.cs, JxlWeightedPredictor.cs,
  JxlGroupHeaderReader.cs and the corresponding Model/IO headers:
  lib/jxl/modular/encoding/dec_ma.cc, encoding.cc, encoding.h, context_predict.h
- Codestream/Modular/JxlModularImage.cs, JxlTransformReader.cs, Transforms/,
  Codestream/Model/JxlTransform.cs and Codestream/Buffers/JxlChannel.cs:
  lib/jxl/modular/modular_image.cc, transform/transform.cc, rct.cc, palette.cc,
  palette.h, squeeze.cc, squeeze.h, squeeze_params.cc
- Codestream/Frames decode/session/section components and
  Codestream/Rendering/JxlFrameRenderer.cs:
  lib/jxl/dec_frame.cc, dec_modular.cc, dec_cache.cc
- Codestream/Metadata/JxlIccStream.cs: lib/jxl/icc_codec.cc, icc_codec_common.cc
  (ICC stream decoding and UnpredictICC)
- Codestream/VarDct/JxlAcStrategy.cs: lib/jxl/ac_strategy.cc, ac_strategy.h,
  ac_context.h, coeff_order.cc, coeff_order.h, entropy_coder.cc
- Codestream/VarDct/JxlQuantWeights.cs: lib/jxl/quant_weights.cc, quant_weights.h
- Codestream/VarDct/JxlDct.cs and JxlDctTables.cs: lib/jxl/dct-inl.h,
  dct_scales.h, dec_transforms-inl.h, transpose-inl.h
- Codestream/VarDct/JxlVarDct.cs and JxlEpfSigma.cs: lib/jxl/dec_group.cc,
  dec_modular.cc, compressed_dc.cc, quantizer.cc, quantizer-inl.h,
  chroma_from_luma.cc, chroma_from_luma.h, passes_state.cc, dec_frame.cc
- Codestream/Rendering/JxlRenderPipeline.cs and Codestream/Buffers/JxlPlane.cs:
  lib/jxl/render_pipeline/simple_render_pipeline.cc, stage_chroma_upsampling.cc,
  stage_gaborish.cc, stage_epf.cc, stage_upsampling.cc, lib/jxl/epf.cc
- Codestream/Color/JxlColorOutput.cs: lib/jxl/dec_xyb.cc, dec_xyb-inl.h,
  render_pipeline/stage_xyb.cc, stage_ycbcr.cc, stage_from_linear.cc,
  cms/transfer_functions-inl.h, cms/jxl_cms_internal.h, cms/opsin_params.h,
  base/matrix_ops.h
- Codestream/Numerics/JxlFastMath.cs: lib/jxl/base/fast_math-inl.h,
  base/rational_polynomial-inl.h
- Codestream/Frames/JxlPatchReader.cs, Codestream/Model/JxlPatch.cs and
  Codestream/Rendering/JxlPatches.cs: lib/jxl/dec_patch_dictionary.cc,
  blending.cc, alpha.cc
- Codestream/Color/JxlDither.cs: lib/jxl/render_pipeline/stage_write.cc
- Codestream/Encoding/JxlBitWriter.cs and JxlFieldWriter.cs: lib/jxl/enc_bit_writer.cc,
  fields.cc (U32/U64/F16 coders)
- Codestream/Encoding/JxlHeaderWriter.cs: lib/jxl/headers.cc, image_metadata.cc,
  color_encoding_internal.cc, frame_header.cc, loop_filter.cc, toc.cc (field order)
- Codestream/Encoding/JxlEntropyEncoder.cs: lib/jxl/enc_ans.cc (histogram coding,
  ANS state machine, hybrid integer tokens), enc_cluster.cc, enc_context_map.cc
- Codestream/Encoding/JxlTreeLearner.cs: lib/jxl/modular/encoding/enc_ma.cc
  (FindBestSplit, property quantization)
- Codestream/Encoding/JxlModularEncoder.cs: lib/jxl/enc_modular.cc,
  modular/encoding/enc_encoding.cc (TokenizeTree), modular/transform/enc_rct.cc,
  enc_palette.cc
- Codestream/Metadata/JxlIccStream.cs (Predict): lib/jxl/enc_icc_codec.cc
- Container/JxlContainerWriter.cs: lib/jxl/encode.cc (box order)
